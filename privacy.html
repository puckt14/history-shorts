Privacy policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

History Shorts Uploader ("the app") is a private tool used only by the owner of the History Shorts YouTube channel.

What the app accesses

The app requests one Google permission: youtube.upload, to upload and schedule videos to the owner's own YouTube channel. It does not read, store or share any other Google account data.

What the app stores

An OAuth refresh token is stored locally on the owner's computer so uploads can run automatically. It is never sent to any third party. No data about other users is collected, because the app has no other users.

Use of Google data

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube API Services are used under the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
